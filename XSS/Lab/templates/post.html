Is your mind controlling you?

We often think that we are in control of our thoughts, but the reality is that our minds can be heavily influenced by external factors, subconscious habits, and cognitive biases. In this modern era of constant digital stimulation, regaining control requires conscious effort and mindfulness.

Understanding the mechanisms behind our decision-making processes is the first step toward taking back the steering wheel. Remember to pause, reflect, and evaluate why you feel a certain way before reacting.
